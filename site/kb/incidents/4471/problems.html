parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Day-first tenants write 07/04, the parser reads 4 July. The day and month swap. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and most of its reports.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports sent through the booking-v3 form, from day-first tenants. The parser fix does not touch it: the form needs its hint changed. Intake tags miss it both ways, so place these reports by tenant and channel, not by tag.

one-day-move: dates off by exactly one day

A flipped day and month cannot move a date by one day. These reports describe something else: the same meeting on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, every June booking a day off, an invite that arrived before its booking. That points at time zones or daylight saving, not at date order. The desk read them this way already. They stay counted where they are filed, under parser, and are tracked on their own.

How to place a new report

  1. Read the body. A date off by one day is one-day-move; add its id to desk-notes.json.
  2. Otherwise look up the tenant: reports through the booking-v3 form are hint.
  3. Everything else from a day-first tenant is parser.

Do not merge on subject line alone (decision 0001): all three problems arrive under the same subjects.
